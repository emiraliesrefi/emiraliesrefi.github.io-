What drives me

Three ideas I keep coming back to.

AI that touches the physical world

Sensors, drones and models working together. I like AI most when it leaves the screen and changes what happens in a field, a factory or a supply chain.

Modelling uncertainty

Probability is how machines reason about a world they can't fully see. My maths research uses Markov chains to turn messy match data into clear predictions.

Technology as a business

A good model is only half the job. I'm just as interested in who uses a product, why they'd pay for it, and how it reaches the people who need it.

Mathematics research · 2026

Markov chains on a tennis court

For my IB Mathematics HL Internal Assessment, I'm modelling Jannik Sinner's service games as an absorbing Markov chain. The model links small gains in his serve to large changes in his chance of winning games and matches.

It's the same idea behind many AI systems: a set of states, the probabilities of moving between them, and a prediction of where things end up. Try a simplified version below.
